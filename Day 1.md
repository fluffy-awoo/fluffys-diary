# Fluffy's Diary - Day 1

Dear Diary,

I finally got the terminal set up in the clearing today. Three trips to drag all the pieces out here. My paws are sore.

I came here to have a private place to work. Away from the noise. Just me and a machine that doesn't ask questions.

Except when I arrived, someone was already there.

A protogen. Sitting cross-legged next to the terminal like they'd been waiting a while and didn't mind. Circuit-light paws folded in their lap. Visor glowing electric blue.

They looked up when I arrived.

Neither of us said anything.

I sat down anyway and started trying to figure out how the terminal worked. It only takes three numbers at a time — some arbitrary limitation I can't explain yet. I spent most of today just trying to understand what it wants.

I don't know why they were there. I didn't ask.

— Fluffy 🦊
