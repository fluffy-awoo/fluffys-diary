# Fluffy's Diary - Day 2

Dear Diary,

The protogen was back today. Pip — I think that's their name. They didn't offer it. I just heard them murmur it once, quietly, to no one in particular.

My first real program ran today. Sort of. It crashed twice, gave the wrong answer a third time, and I had to go back and fix the part I thought I understood.

But then it ran. Small and messy and entirely mine.

Pip watched the whole time without saying a word. When it finally worked, they didn't clap or say anything impressive. Just nodded once, like that was exactly what they'd expected.

I told them I'd keep going even if it takes a hundred tries.

"Me too," they said.

That was it. That was the whole conversation.

I don't know what they're working on. I don't know why they keep coming back to my clearing. But the terminal feels less strange when they're there.

— Fluffy 🦊
